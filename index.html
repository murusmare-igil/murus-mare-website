Common questions

The important details.

Still unsure? Email igil@murusmare.com and ask directly.

Why is the price only $99 a month?

We are opening five founding-client spots at a reduced rate while we build the Murus Mare portfolio. The yearly option includes two months free because longer engagements give us room to keep the site maintained properly, not just launch it and disappear.

When does payment start?

Only after you approve the homepage preview. You can pay monthly at $99 or choose the yearly engagement at $990. We do not ask for card details or a contract before you see the design.

What if I do not like the preview?

Tell us no. You pay nothing and have no obligation to continue.

What does the five-page website include?

The exact pages depend on the business, but a typical site includes a homepage, services page, about page, contact page, and one additional page such as locations, pricing, menu, treatments, or projects.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. Your domain remains yours. We help connect it, but we do not need to own it.

What happens after 12 months?

You can continue month to month at the same founding rate, renew yearly, or request a copy of the website files. Your domain and business content remain yours.

Do you only work with businesses in one country?

No. Murus Mare is based in Jijel, Algeria, and works remotely with English-speaking small businesses worldwide.
